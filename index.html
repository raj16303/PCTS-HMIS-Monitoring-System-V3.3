PCTS & HMIS Monitoring System

Office of Block Chief Medical & Health Officer, Banera (Bhilwara)

Enterprise Government MIS Platform for HMIS monthly monitoring & reporting.

Developed By: RS Solanki, IA
Department: Medical & Health Department
Office: Block Chief Medical Health Officer, Banera
Version: 1.1.0
The tool automatically parses any HMIS export, remaps indicators, and produces District / Block / Sector / Facility / Indicator compile reports with dynamic filters, print-friendly formatting, and detailed PDF exports. All processing runs locally in the browser.
